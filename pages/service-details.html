Mosquito barrier treatment

Our core treatment: a protective coat where mosquitoes rest, renewed before it wears off.

How the barrier works

Adult mosquitoes spend the day resting in cool, shady foliage. A barrier treatment coats those surfaces — the undersides of leaves, dense shrubs, under decks and along fences — so mosquitoes are knocked down when they land, long before they find you on the patio.

Each visit also removes or treats standing water so new mosquitoes don’t hatch. Together that typically cuts biting activity by 85–95% within the first two visits.

Safety information

  • EPA-registered products applied at label rates by state-licensed technicians.
  • Kids and pets can return once dry — about 30 minutes. We text you.
  • No spraying of edible gardens, open blooms, ponds or play surfaces.
  • Product labels and safety data sheets available in your dashboard.
