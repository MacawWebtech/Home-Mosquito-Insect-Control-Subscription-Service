About YardShield

We started YardShield so families could stop planning summer around mosquitoes.

Our story

Pest control that runs itself

In 2016 our founder, Elena, was calling a different exterminator every few weeks and still couldn’t sit on her own porch at dusk. The problem wasn’t the spray — it was the schedule. Mosquito treatments only work if they’re repeated before the last one wears off.

So we built a subscription around that rhythm: one inspection, a season of automatic visits, and a dashboard that shows you every treatment. Today we protect more than 48,000 yards across six metro areas.

Our mission

Fewer bites, not fewer bees

We target the pests that bother you and leave pollinators alone. That means no blanket spraying on flowers, treatments timed away from bloom, and technicians trained to spot what not to treat.

  • Pollinator-aware application on every visit
  • Lowest effective dose, never more
  • Plant-based options on request
